# Code of Conduct

Dispatch is for people building useful, private tools for teams and agents. We want
everyone who takes part to be able to do it without being harassed or talked down to.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be specific and kind in reviews: say what is wrong, why, and what would help.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here was a beginner at something.
- Keep discussion on topic, and keep private information (yours and other people's) out of
  issues, logs and screenshots.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about anyone, including on the basis of
  identity, background, experience or opinions.
- Sexualised language or attention, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption, spam, or personal attacks in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who
do not follow this code, and will explain the decision when it is useful to do so.

To report a problem, open an issue titled "Conduct report" that contains no details. A
maintainer will arrange a private channel. If the problem involves a maintainer, say so in
that issue and ask for a different contact. Reports are handled with discretion.

## Scope

This applies to the project's repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
space where someone represents the project.
